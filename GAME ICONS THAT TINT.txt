GAME ICONS THAT TINT
====================

For the "bubble" readout's "sprite" key, and for anything else in Weapon Forge
that tints a sprite.

WHY THIS LIST EXISTS
--------------------

A UI tint is a MULTIPLY. So "color" can only ever DARKEN art that already has
colour in it - a blue icon can be made navy, never yellow. Only art that is
PURE WHITE can be tinted to any hue you like at full brightness.

That makes "which of the game's sprites are white" a real question with a real
answer, and guessing it wrong looks like the tint is broken rather than like the
sprite was a bad choice.

Every icon-sized sprite in PUNK v0.12.9 was checked pixel by pixel: 942 of them
between 8x8 and 64x64 with enough opaque pixels to see. EXACTLY 111 ARE PURE
WHITE and are listed below. Everything not on this list will darken rather than
recolour.

NO ART IS REPRODUCED HERE - these are names only. The sprites belong to the
game; this file just says which ones behave.

START HERE INSTEAD
------------------

Weapon Forge ships TEN icons drawn for this feature, in "Example Sprites":

    wf_alert  wf_warn  wf_heat  wf_lock  wf_bolt
    wf_star   wf_ring  wf_dot   wf_plus  wf_skull

They are drawn white-on-alpha on purpose, they are sized and shaped to read at
a glance as a status icon, and they are ours - so they work, they tint, and
they are in the repo. Reach for the list below only when you want something
specific the shipped set does not cover.

A WARNING ABOUT SIZES
---------------------

These range from 8x8 to 37x36 and the bubble's slot is a fixed 1.35 world
units, so a big sprite and a small one will NOT sit the same. Use "scale".
