==============================================================================
PUNK - EVERY SOUND IN THE GAME
==============================================================================

Generated from the game's own Audio Database for Weapon Forge and Module
Forge. Names and ids only - no audio is copied here.

  259 sounds declared
  189 have at least one clip and will actually play
  70 ARE DECLARED AND EMPTY - naming one gives you SILENCE

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW TO USE ONE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Put the NAME, with its slashes, in any sound field:

    "weapon": { "shootSfx": "Weapons/Shotgun/Shoot" }
    "impactSfx": "Cells/Tuff"
    "charm": { "sfx": "Enemies/Cross/Death" }

Matching is case-insensitive. A raw id still works if you have one.

The lookup order is CUSTOM FIRST, then the game: a file of your own in the
'sounds' folder with the same name wins. That is the same order every
sprite key uses.

EVERY SLOT THAT TAKES A SOUND. Read out of the builder rather than
remembered, because the list had two mistakes in it when I wrote it from
memory:

  in "weapon":   shootSfx    reloadSfx    startSfx    releaseSfx
                 warmupSfx   continousShootSfx

  top level:     impactSfx

  "sfx" inside:  crit    leech    lunge    hitSound    swipe    rally
                 charm   execute  tractor  onKill      gas

  named slots:   charge.chargeSfx      charge.readySfx
                 overheat.overheatSfx  overheat.recoverSfx
                 catalyst.triggerSfx   catalyst.armSfx
                 shield.raiseSfx       shield.ramSfx
                 shield.blockSfx

Use "none" - or silent / silence / off / mute / "-" - in any of them to
switch a sound OFF.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
READ THIS BEFORE PICKING ONE
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

EMPTY ENTRIES ARE THE TRAP. 70 of the 259 are declared in the database with
no clip attached. They resolve perfectly and play nothing, so the weapon
looks broken rather than misconfigured. They are marked EMPTY below and the
build log warns if you name one.

MULTI-CLIP ENTRIES PICK ONE AT RANDOM. A sound with 10 clips sounds
different every time it plays, which is what makes terrain and pickups feel
alive. A weapon firing 8 times a second on a 1-clip sound sounds like a
machine; on a 10-clip one it sounds like a gun. Clip counts are listed.

30-CLIP RESOURCE SOUNDS ARE A LADDER, not variation. Resources/Fuel, Caps,
Purple, Tech, Money and Stamina each hold 30 clips that climb the scale as
you chain pickups. Firing a weapon with one of those will walk up the
ladder, which is either delightful or a mess.

LOOPING ENTRIES ARE FOR THE HELD SLOTS ONLY - continousShootSfx and
warmupSfx. The game starts those, keeps the handle and stops them later;
a one-shot there plays once and never restarts, and a looping sound in a
one-shot slot never stops.

2D entries have no position - they play at the same volume wherever they
happen. Right for UI, wrong for a gun.

Volume is the database's own value and is left alone; a quiet entry is
quiet on purpose (the hitmarker is 0.22).
